OrbitDrop

1. Drag OrbitDrop into Applications.
2. Open it from Applications. Look for the circular menu-bar icon.
3. Drag an image or PDF in Finder, then drop it on the wheel.

Hold Option while dragging for Crop, Add BG, and other tools.
Click the center to switch wheels when opening a file from the menu.
Drag the center to move the wheel. Escape dismisses it.

Files are processed on your Mac. Originals are preserved.
OrbitDrop has no Dock icon. Quit it from its menu-bar menu.

Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later.
This personal build is locally signed, not Apple-notarized. If macOS
blocks it, use its Open Anyway option in Privacy & Security.
